Committees & Agendas

1. United Nations Security Council (UNSC)

Chair: Mr. Aditya Kiran

Agenda: Reassessing the legality and security implications of counter-terrorism operations conducted without host state consent.

2. United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC)

Chair: Adv. Shivam Jain Kakadia

Agenda: Addressing human rights violations due to police and state intervention during protests and peaceful assemblies.

3. United Nations Commission on the Status of Women (UNCSW)

Chair: Mr. Annsh H Gharrat

Agenda: Strengthening global frameworks to prevent sexual exploitation and trafficking of women and girls, with emphasis on accountability and survivor-centric justice.

4. Lok Sabha

Speaker: Mr. Suraj Subhash Tondaio
Deputy Speaker: Mr. Jainee Chauhan

Agenda: National security and civil liberties — examination of sedition laws, UAPA, and preventive detention provisions.

5. International Press (IP)

Chair: Mr. Vishnu Nair

Agenda: Coverage and reporting of proceedings across all committees.
